## About Section

### Short bio (hero/subtitle)

### Full bio (About section)

I started at MTBank in Belarus — commercial lending, then product management, then leading customer experience initiatives across the bank. Internet banking, mobile apps, loyalty programs, CRM automation. Six years learning how financial institutions build and ship digital products.

After moving to Canada, I pivoted into consulting. At Aequilibrium, I worked as a BA and Product Owner on Backbase mobile banking for credit unions across Canada. At JUDI.AI, I led product development for a SaaS lending platform. Since 2020, I've been at Modes as Director of Customer Success — delivering Backbase and Salesforce implementations, acting as the bridge between vendors and client product teams, and coaching new consultants into the space.

On the side, I teach MBA courses at University Canada West — Business Analytics and Digital Transformation. I hold an MBA from UBC Sauder, a Master of Finance, and CBAP and CSPO designations.

## Scoutloop Section

**Title:** Scoutloop

**Tagline:** Know Before You Go

**Description:**
Scoutloop is a pre-trip briefing layer for backcountry users in BC. It aggregates official and crowdsourced conditions — weather, avalanche danger, road closures, fire bans, air quality — into a single briefing tailored to your destination. Built by backcountry enthusiasts in BC, for backcountry enthusiasts in BC.

**Features:**
- Smart Briefing — Official ECCC weather, DriveBC road events, and BC Wildfire data in one view
- AI Verdicts — Gemini-powered risk assessment and safety recommendations
- Offline Maps — Automatic offline map generation for trip destinations
- Telegram Bot — Real-time scout reports and alerts

**URL:** scoutloop.me

**Tech:** Next.js, Supabase, Vercel
